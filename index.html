❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What size portable power station do I need for home backup?

For small electronics and smartphones, a 250Wh to 500Wh unit is plenty. If your goal is to keep a refrigerator running during an emergency storm outage, aim for a minimum of 1000Wh to 2000Wh with at least 1500W continuous pure sine wave inverter output.

How long do LiFePO4 power station batteries last?

Modern LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate) battery chemistry provides 3,000 to 4,000 full recharge cycles to 80% original capacity. That equates to nearly 10 years of regular use compared to standard Lithium-ion batteries which degrade after 500 cycles.

Can I keep a power station continuously plugged into the wall?

Most premium stations featured in our reviews include pass-through charging and EPS/UPS modes, allowing you to use them as an uninterrupted emergency power backup for desktop computers and medical devices.
